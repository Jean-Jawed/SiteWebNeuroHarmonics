About

Who's behind NeuroHarmonics?

One developer, one app, and the wish to explain honestly what it does.

NeuroHarmonics is developed by Jawed Tahir, a developer based in Marseille, France, passionate about technology and its impact on well-being.

The approach

Make binaural beats simple to access: no subscription, no account and no data collection. The app does one thing, and this site explains how it works and what you can reasonably expect from it.

Contact

A question, a suggestion, a bug? Get in touch through the contact form. The author's other projects are on javed.fr.
